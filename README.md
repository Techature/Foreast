# 🌿 Foreast
 
> *"Stay on the path. The forest is watching."*
 
## What is Foreast?
 
Foreast is a 2D nature-themed maze adventure built as a single, self-contained HTML file.
You guide a little green heart through a winding forest trail using your mouse — no buttons,
no typing, just pure hand-eye coordination. The path is flanked by swaying grass, marked
by dark mountain peaks, and guarded by sharp magenta thorn-bushes.
 
It runs entirely in the browser. No installs, no internet needed — just open and play.
 
## Why does it exist?
 
Foreast was made for kids aged 8–12 to build critical thinking and spatial awareness
in a gentle, nature-inspired way. The maze encourages planning ahead, staying focused,
and learning that it's perfectly okay to try again (or even grab a pencil and paper!).
 
The lose screen doesn't scold you — it invites you to print the maze and solve it by hand.
Because sometimes the best thinking happens away from a screen.
 
## How does it help?
 
- 🧠 Builds spatial reasoning and path-planning skills
- 🖱️ Trains hand-eye coordination through mouse-drag movement
- 🌱 Introduces natural consequences (thorn = life lost) without frustration
- 📄 Encourages offline, paper-based problem solving as a valid alternative
- 💚 Keeps anxiety low with a calm colour palette and forgiving 2-life system
 
## How to play
 
1. Open `foreast.html` in any modern browser
2. Hold down your left mouse button on the green heart
3. Drag it carefully along the dark green corridor
4. Avoid the magenta thorn-bushes (they cost a life!)
5. Mountain peaks slow you down — cross them carefully
6. Reach the golden goal at the top-right to win 🌟
 
## Tech Stack
 
- Vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — zero dependencies
- HTML5 Canvas for terrain, obstacles, and the heart
- Single file, deployable anywhere (GitHub Pages, local, USB drive)
